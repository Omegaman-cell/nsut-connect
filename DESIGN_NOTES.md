# Design and copy revision

The first interface had too many slogans, coloured panels and repeated prompts. The revised app uses plain headings, neutral surfaces and one muted green accent. Colours indicate selection or an action; topic names remain text labels. Theme values live together at the start of App.css.

## References reviewed

| Reference | Observation | Change in this app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Linear's designer-led UI refresh](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui) | Its designers describe reducing visual noise, improving hierarchy and using more neutral light/dark themes. | Shared semantic colour variables, quieter navigation, higher-contrast text. |
| [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions) | Titles, authors, categories and activity carry the discussion view. | The feed gives posts priority; the promotional banner and reminder panel were removed. |
| [Published examples of Claude-built sites](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-built-9-websites-in-under-an-hour-with-claude-heres-how-its-possible) | The examples include gradients, decorative animation, glowing interactions and inspirational copy. Those choices suit some demonstrations but do not help a campus discussion tool. | No decorative effects; controls and notices stay focused on the task. |
| [Wikipedia's AI-writing field guide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) | It describes promotional wording, vague connections and repeated rhetorical patterns. This is editorial guidance, not proof of authorship. | Direct labels and specific explanations replace the original slogans. |
